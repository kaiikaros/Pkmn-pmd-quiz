Campaign Pre-Game Survey

Welcome! Before we kick off Session 1, please complete this quick 15-question survey as I set stuff up.

1. Your teacher assigns a major group project, but lets you choose your role. Which do you grab first?
2. A delivery package with no name or return address arrives at your front door. What do you do?
3. You're walking home from school or work and it starts pouring rain, but you forgot an umbrella. What’s your move?
4. Two of your close friends get into a massive, petty argument in your group chat. How do you respond?
5. You bought a birthday present for a friend. How do you wrap it?
6. You drop your phone, picking it up to see a giant crack across the screen. What is your reaction?
7. After a long, exhausting week of school or work, how do you prefer to spend your Friday evening?
8. You’re playing a multiplayer video game or board game with friends. What's your play style?
9. The power goes out completely in your house late at night. What’s your first move?
10. A friend confides in you about a crush or a personal dilemma. How do you handle it?
11. If you won a free vacation anywhere in the world, where would you go?
12. You spot an unfamiliar, stray cat or dog wandering down your street. What do you do?
13. You overslept and have 10 minutes to get out the door for school or work. What do you do?
14. You walk into a crowded room full of strangers at an event. Where do you gravitate?
15. You and your friends/family are going on a long weekend road trip in one car. What position do you naturally take?

Responses Submitted!

Thank you for filling out the campaign survey. Your responses have been recorded by the DM.

Sit back and relax, the session will start shortly!
